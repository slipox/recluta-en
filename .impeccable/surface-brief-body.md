## Scope and visitor mode

Primary surface: the installed PWA's main screen (`index.html`). Mode: Operate — the user completes a daily training task, not a marketing read.

## Audience, job, action

Hispanic adult learning English at home with a military-style program. Job: start today's session, run a timed drill, record evidence, and see the itinerary's remaining legs. Primary action: the perforated "INICIAR SESIÓN" stub on the current week's coupon.

## Constraints

Offline after first load; all data local; no backend, no API keys, no external fonts or images at runtime; touch targets ≥44px; Spanish UI with English target phrases; no fabricated proficiency claims.

## Chosen direction

Jet-age airline ticket wallet: crisp white coupon stock, carrier navy and red, carbon-purple filled figures, hairline rules, tabbed coupon heads, red VOID-style stamps for cancelled states. This is an itinerary of 10 legs; a completed session tears off a stub, nothing disappears — it cancels. Raised with: one type size per block (from the timetable rack), a phase color strip decoded per active coupon (from the Saville catalog), and a 1px day-state rail (from the centre-rail reference page).

## Memorable moment

The current week's coupon fills the first viewport with its route arc, fixed carbon fields and a detachable stub. Pressing "INICIAR SESIÓN" tears the stub once and stamps the coupon COMPLETADO; the wallet below shows the remaining legs.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A training plan as a ticket wallet rather than a dashboard of progress rings — the artifact itself carries sequence, state and consequence.

OWN-WORLD: Unbleached coupon white; carrier navy #16263F; signal red #C4262E; carbon purple #3B356B for filled figures; pale rule #C9C3B4; tabbed heads in condensed caps; hairline boxes; perforation dots; rotated rectangular stamps; one ink size per field with weight and case doing the ranking.

STORY: The user sees a route with 10 legs, understands that each completed day cancels a stub, and acts immediately on the current coupon. The wallet always shows what remains, so the chain is visible.
